14 / FAQ

Questions, answered.

How many templates are there now?

100 templates, arranged into 10 sections with 10 templates per section.

How does an individual download work?

DEWIFY generates the selected standalone HTML file in the browser and downloads it as a normal .html file.

How do the new packs work?

The themed packs are generated into ZIP downloads in the browser, so the repository does not need to store duplicate copies of every template.

What happened to the old template pack?

It is now the Classic Pack.

Where is DTA?

DTA is one of the main DEWIFY products. Use the DTA section above or open the dedicated DTA product page for the expanded details.
